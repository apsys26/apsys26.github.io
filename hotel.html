Venue

APSys 2026 is held at Bangkok Marriott Marquis Queen’s Park, located in the heart of Bangkok, providing easy access to the city's attractions and business districts.

Workshop location

The Sala Thai — Ballroom

5th floor of the hotel

Registration, the keynote, all technical sessions, the coffee breaks and both poster sessions take place here. Lunch on both days is served at Goji Kitchen, on the hotel’s ground floor.

  • By elevator Take either the north or south tower elevator to the 4th floor, then the escalator up one more floor to the ballroom.
  • By escalator From the ground floor, behind the concierge desk, take the escalators up to the 5th floor.
